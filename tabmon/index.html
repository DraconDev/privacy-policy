TabMon Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

TabMon is a browser extension that runs a creature-collecting game while you browse. It has no TabMon account, analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting. Its game code loads sounds and images from the installed extension.

Information TabMon uses

  • Collection and settings. TabMon reads and saves your creatures, custom creature names, catch dates, progress, training, and ready squad. It also saves settings such as sound, appearance, spawn controls, and the first-run flag. These records use the browser's extension sync storage. If browser sync is enabled for this data, your browser provider can sync it to your account and other eligible profiles. Extra collection entries can be held on this device when the synced record is full. Sync storage may briefly hold a random marker to prevent duplicate collection writes. The marker carries no battle details.
  • Website information. The extension runs a content script so it can show the encounter cue. It reads the current page URL and whether the page is visible or focused to decide where the cue can appear and apply your blocklist. It keeps the last focused HTTP or HTTPS site's origin on this device. An origin includes the scheme, hostname, and any port. TabMon does not store the page path, query, or fragment in that record. It does not read page content, forms, cookies, or browsing history.
  • Local game state. Your site blocklist, current encounter and spawn timing, saved battles and reward recovery records, catch markers, and audio resume position stay in the local browser profile. TabMon does not send that data to its developer.

Backups and controls

The export button saves a collection backup as a JSON file. You can restore that file in TabMon or share it yourself. TabMon does not upload it. You can manage browser sync through your browser, edit the blocklist in Settings, release creatures from your collection, and remove locally stored extension data by uninstalling TabMon.

Children

TabMon is not directed at children under 13.

TabMon support

For help with TabMon or questions about this policy, email draconwin@gmail.com. Include your browser and TabMon version if you are reporting a problem.
